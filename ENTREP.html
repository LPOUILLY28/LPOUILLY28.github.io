NeauMade: Atmospheric Water Generator

Co-founder & Engineer for a startup project aiming to solve water scarcity in Sub-Saharan Africa.

Top 5 Finalist - Les Entrep's Competition

The Concept: Water from Air

Addressing the critical lack of drinking water affecting 408 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa, we developed NeauMade. It is an autonomous Atmospheric Water Generator (AWG) designed to extract moisture from the air (ideal for the 80% humidity of the Congo) and convert it into pure drinking water, independent of traditional infrastructure.

Value Proposition:
  • - Capacity: 20 Liters of clean water per day
  • - Autonomy: Solar-powered option for off-grid areas
  • - Target Market: Rural households & NGOs in DRC

Engineering & Product Design

As the engineer of the group, I led the technical design of the system. The process involves drawing ambient air via a fan, cooling it through a condenser to reach the dew point, and collecting the condensate. The water then passes through a multi-stage filtration system to ensure WHO quality standards.

Technical Architecture:
  • - Air Filtration: Anti-static & HEPA filters
  • - Water Treatment: UV Sterilization + Ozone Generator
  • - Structure: Aluminum 5754 chassis (corrosion resistant)
  • - Cost Engineering: BOM optimization to reach €507 production cost

Project Vision & Demonstration

To effectively communicate our vision to investors, we created a promotional video showcasing the product's capabilities. The video highlights the key pillars of NeauMade: Mobility, Efficiency, and Autonomy. It demonstrates the potential impact on daily life in rural areas, emphasizing how our solar-powered solution can transform air into a vital resource anywhere.

Business Strategy & Financials

We developed a viable business model based on a B2B2C strategy, partnering with NGOs and local distributors. The financial analysis projected a break-even point in Year 1 with 70 units sold. This comprehensive approach led us to the Top 5 in the regional competition.

Financial Key Metrics:
  • - Selling Price: €800 (Competitive vs €2000 market avg)
  • - Gross Margin: ~36.5% per unit
  • - Market Size: $2.8 Billion Global AWG Market
  • - Legal Structure: SASU (Flexible management)
