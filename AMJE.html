About AMJE Lille

AMJE (Arts et Métiers Junior-Entreprise) is the student-run consultancy association of Arts et Métiers ParisTech. As a Junior-Enterprise, we provide engineering and business consulting services to real companies while gaining professional experience.

Operating as a fully functional business, AMJE connects students with industry partners, delivering high-quality technical studies and strategic solutions. Every project is an opportunity to apply classroom theory to tangible business challenges.

Key Responsibilities:
  • - Business Development & Client Prospection
  • - Project Management & Client Relations
  • - Technical Study Coordination with Engineering Students
