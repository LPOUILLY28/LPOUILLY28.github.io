Sherco SM-R: High-Performance Conversion

An engineering project transforming a standard 50cc motorcycle into a 70cc competition-ready machine through extensive powertrain and chassis modifications.

Powertrain Engineering (70cc Racing)

The core of the project involved replacing the standard AM6 Euro 4 engine components to support high-RPM performance. I installed a Bidalot RF-WR 70cc cylinder kit paired with a reinforced crankshaft to handle the increased load. The intake and exhaust systems were completely redesigned to optimize airflow dynamics.

Engine Configuration:
  • - Top End: Bidalot RF-WR 70cc Kit
  • - Bottom End: Bidalot RF-WR Replica Crankshaft + 4-Disc Racing Clutch
  • - Intake: Stage 6 R/T 28mm Carb, V-Force Reeds, Most 10mm Wedge
  • - Electronics: MVT Digital Direct (DD) Inner Rotor Ignition
  • - Exhaust: Malossi MHR Supermoto (Tuned for 70cc)

Chassis & Suspension Dynamics

To match the upgraded engine power, I overhauled the rolling chassis for better stability and handling. The stock suspension was replaced with a stiffer Inverted Fork (R16V) sourced from a Rieju MRT Trophy. I also integrated anodized wheels to reduce unsprung weight and improve the aesthetic.

🔧 Cycle Parts Upgrades:
  • - Front Suspension: R16V Inverted Fork (MRT Trophy Spec)
  • - Wheels: Blue Anodized Spoked Rims (Derbi DRD Racing)
  • - Aesthetics: Bud Racing Graphic Kit

Project Evolution

This project required not only mechanical skills for assembly but also troubleshooting capabilities to tune the carburetor jetting and ignition timing. The transformation from the stock Euro 4 setup to the final "Full Bidalot" configuration resulted in a significant increase in horsepower and a unique custom look.
